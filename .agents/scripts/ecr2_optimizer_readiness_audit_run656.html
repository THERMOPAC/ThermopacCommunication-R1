13. Objective and temperature treatment

No governed optimizer objective currently exists. Possible future objectives—minimize height, diameter, energy, or RRBO loss while meeting aromatic and sulfur specifications—remain candidate concepts only.

An optimizer cannot safely rank run 656 outputs because the physical transfer response is preliminary, sulfur is unavailable, no flooding limit is implemented, no economic model is persisted, and the product-quality basis is not fully resolved.

Temperature conclusion. Temperature is not optimizer-ready. The 70 °C equilibrium result relies on NRTL extrapolation from a 25 °C calibration and is explicitly pending validation against controlled RRBO/NMP LLE data near the operating temperature.

The C2 theoretical stage result must remain separate from ECR-2 physical compartment semantics. C2 fractional NT = 0.82802, rounded NT = 1, does not mean that the ECR-2 column has one physical compartment or one validated transfer stage.

14. Grid independence

Grid independence is not demonstrated.

  • Run 656 uses three numerical compartments.
  • No persisted same-operating-point comparison across refined compartment grids was found.
  • Existing tests verify BVP mechanics, balances, and regression behavior.
  • Those tests do not establish outlet, recovery, or objective independence from compartment count.

A future grid audit must compare the same operating point across multiple compartment counts, including outlet compositions, recovery, transfer, d₃₂/holdup profiles, residuals, and all objective-relevant quantities.

17. Fail-closed conditions

The optimizer must represent the following as INFEASIBLE, never as merely poor objective values:

  • Unsupported phase orientation
  • Missing/unapproved C2 basis
  • Missing partition basis
  • Missing d₃₂ configuration
  • Invalid flow vector
  • Invalid physical MWs
  • Invalid local composition
  • Failed property closure
  • Failed NRTL flash
  • Invalid holdup or d₃₂
  • Invalid interfacial area/slip
  • Invalid transfer volume
  • Donor-inventory violation
  • Negative/non-finite state
  • BVP non-convergence
  • Failed component/total balance

Missing evidence, out-of-scope correlation applicability, and an unavailable requested sulfur/DBT constraint must also produce a hard infeasibility state.

18. Optimizer-exploit audit and validation requirements

Principal exploit risks

  1. Lower diameter could improve apparent d₃₂ through the preliminary turbulence equation.
  2. Higher S/O could appear beneficial without a solvent-cost, capacity, or flooding constraint.
  3. The preliminary d₃₂ coefficient range could be exploited as a hidden decision variable.
  4. C2 NT = 1 could be misread as an ECR-2 physical stage count.
  5. Numerical compartment count could be varied to create a numerical optimum.
  6. Assumed MWs, diffusivities, and viscosities could be treated as measured facts.
  7. Numerical convergence could be mistaken for validated transfer performance.
  8. Sulfur compliance could be claimed even though sulfur/DBT is not modeled.

Required validation before optimizer use

  • Controlled RRBO/NMP tie-lines at or near 70 °C, including water and purity effects.
  • Validation of local partition and NRTL behavior against measured system data.
  • Pilot d₃₂ measurements versus rpm, rotor/column geometry, power, and phase loading.
  • Holdup, flooding margin, residence-time, and axial-mixing validation.
  • Component mass-transfer coefficient and rate measurements.
  • Temperature-dependent density, viscosity, interfacial tension, MW, and diffusivity evidence.
  • Sulfur/DBT extraction and product-quality model validation.
  • Grid-independence evidence using persisted same-point refined-grid comparisons.
