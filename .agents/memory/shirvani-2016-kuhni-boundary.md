description: Source-audit verdict for the paper's drop-size and flooding correlations.
---

Shirvani 2016 Eq. 5 is dimensionally structured but not admissible: the paper never defines its epsilon geometry term or required numerical unit convention, and coherent-SI replay does not reproduce the plotted drop sizes or claimed error.

Eq. 7 passes approximate source-figure replay and is mathematically diameter-scalable through continuous and dispersed superficial velocities. It has no direct diameter, rotor-diameter, or compartment-height term, so every geometry beyond the fixed 117 mm source apparatus is extrapolated.

For governed RRBO/wet-NMP, dispersed viscosity is about 144–182 times the source range and enters Eq. 7 with an exponent near -10. The resulting flooding prediction is physically unusable, so Eq. 7 cannot establish the governing diameter basis despite being a source-backed candidate.

The paper reports mutually inconsistent errors: drop size is 7.2% in the abstract but 5.70% in Table 6; flooding appears as 5.7%, 11.54–11.56%, and 7.2% in different sections/tables.

**Why:** A published and dimensionally valid empirical equation can still be unfit for extrapolation when its dominant property ratio is far outside the fitted data or the source cannot reproduce itself.

**How to apply:** Preserve Eq. 7 only as a versioned extrapolating capacity candidate with full source-range diagnostics. Reject physically absurd RRBO/NMP outputs and never select a final diameter from this route alone.
