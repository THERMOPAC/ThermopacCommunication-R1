name: ECR-2 sulfur objective boundary
description: The physical Stage 8 model must treat sulfur removal as the primary product objective, independently of aromatic reduction.
---

The future physics-based pre-pilot ECR-2 thermodynamic cascade must explicitly conserve valuable hydrocarbons and sulfur-bearing polar/heteroaromatic components. Sulfur reduction is not an independent empirical calculation: sulfur travels with each physical sulfur-bearing component, whose LLE partition into NMP determines raffinate sulfur. It must predict selective transfer in every equilibrium stage and physical ECR compartment, with raffinate sulfur as the primary quality specification; hydrocarbon-only aromatics may remain a separate secondary constraint, and RRBO recovery remains independently required. The pre-pilot standard is the best defensible combination of published data, predictive thermodynamics, mechanistic transport/hydrodynamics, numerical methods, optimization, uncertainty analysis, and bounded AI assistance—not a rough calculator merely because pilot data are absent. The eventual Stage 8 architecture must couple this result to mass-transfer/hydrodynamic calculations for \(N_T\), diameter, height, and operating-envelope studies, while preserving future pilot calibration without rebuilding the architecture.

**Why:** The thermodynamic slate has no explicit sulfur species, so aromatic transfer alone cannot support a molecular sulfur-partition claim. A separately governed Stage-1 allocation may nevertheless support bounded post-processing when each allocation share follows the actual retained mass fraction of its assigned SAT/MONO/DI/POLY/PA family.

**How to apply:** Never infer sulfur from total-aromatics removal or add a sulfur-removal factor, efficiency, or empirical aromatic-to-sulfur relationship. The approved 7C-1.4 post-processing route is \(S_R=S_F\sum_i f_{S,i}(m_{i,R}/m_{i,F})\), using only the saved Stage-1 allocation and the accepted cascade's actual component masses. It is calculable only after physical/numerical gates pass and must remain explicitly labelled mass-allocation post-processing, not sulfur-component thermodynamics. A future sulfur-capable physical cascade still requires approved sulfur/polar pseudo-components and partition evidence.
