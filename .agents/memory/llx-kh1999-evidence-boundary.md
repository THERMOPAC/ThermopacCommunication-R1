name: K&H 1999 secondary evidence boundary
description: Secondary reproductions separate the Kühni C1 value from provisional project constants and leave dispersed-side roles unresolved.
---

The 2011 and 2017 peer-reviewed secondary papers reproduce the same K&H
continuous-side Sherwood structure. The 2017 table assigns \(C_1=7.5\) to
Kühni; this is distinct from the project-approved provisional \(C_1=0.90\).
The 2011 dispersed-side structure is reproduced, but no Kühni \(C_2\), regime
selector, characteristic velocity, \(F_c/F_d\), or K&H partition-basis
definition was recovered. The printed two-film relation contains an undefined
dimensionless \(m\).

**Why:** Symbol-name collisions would silently put provisional constants into
the wrong correlation family and enable unsupported transfer-rate predictions.

**How to apply:** Keep the literature report and numerical kernel gated until
the missing Kühni power basis, dispersed-side roles, regime criteria, and
overall partition relation are independently governed.
