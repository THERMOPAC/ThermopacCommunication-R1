name: Oliveira short-Kühni correlation boundary
description: Admission limits for Oliveira 2008 drop-size and holdup regressions in pre-pilot hydrodynamics.
---

Oliveira 2008 Eq. 6 and Eq. 7 are fixed-apparatus regressions for a five-stage, 150 mm short Kühni using water-continuous/Exxsol D-80 and absolute L/min flows. They contain no diameter scaling and must remain separately identified reference candidates, never the default basis for candidate-diameter selection.

Outside the published apparatus, flow, speed, Reynolds, chemistry, or property domain, a finite result may only be reported as `CALCULATED_EXTRAPOLATED` with every violated range. A physically impossible result is rejected rather than labelled extrapolated.

The paper's tabulated water properties reproduce rotor Reynolds numbers of about 6,542–19,626, not its stated 7,225–21,675 range; preserve this source-internal inconsistency in provenance rather than adjusting viscosity.

**Why:** The regressions reproduce the paper's figures but their absolute-flow form cannot scale to governed RRBO/wet-NMP diameters, and RRBO dispersed viscosity is far outside the Exxsol experiment.

**How to apply:** Keep Oliveira Eq. 1/6/7 available only as versioned source-replay or diagnostic candidates. Never use Eq. 7 as flooding/capacity evidence, and never select a diameter from these correlations.
