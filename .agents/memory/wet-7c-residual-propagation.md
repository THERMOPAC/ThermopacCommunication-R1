The exact 3 wt% water daughter states from the full \(N_T=1\ldots10\) run are stable under native seven-component cCOSMO: all 110 TPD minima are numerical zero. Adding the inherited six-component NMP-LLE residual recreates a large negative, uniformly MONO-rich basin in all 110 states. The residual's MONO–NMP block is dominant, with its reference term the largest single contributor.

**Why:** This controlled A/B result distinguishes the old six-component residual-model defect from a new water-driven instability. The residual-added objective was independently replayed at every persisted minimizer and reproduced the saved minima to numerical precision.

**How to apply:** Treat residual-bearing 7C-1.1/1.2 as exact historical replay routes only. New work uses the separately versioned native-cCOSMO route with the 1.2 cascade and thresholds unchanged. It remains research-only and release-ineligible until declared dry and direct water-bearing LLE qualification pass, even though the frozen wet stability replay passes.
