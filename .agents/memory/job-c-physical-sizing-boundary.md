---
name: Job C physical sizing boundary
description: Physical sizing requires candidate-specific transport and supported hardware evidence, not frozen diagnostic geometry.
---

Physical sizing must recalculate film coefficients when candidate d32 or slip changes, search height for that candidate, and revalidate rounded installed geometry. A previously accepted height is not transferable across diameter/RPM candidates.

**Why:** Reusing accepted transport films with new hydraulics combines incompatible physical states and can select a false minimum-volume design.

**How to apply:** Preserve immutable parent lineage and store sizing as a separate child assessment. Missing supported mechanical spacing is an explicit dependency, not permission to equate FV cells with hardware. Theoretical stages divided by physical compartments alone does not establish an admitted efficiency model; leave efficiency unavailable until supported.
