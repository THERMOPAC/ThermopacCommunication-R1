name: Job-C positive-branch qualification
description: Governs how a Job-C branch beyond a numerical solvent boundary becomes reproducible evidence.
---

A pseudo-arclength candidate alone is not a qualified Job-C branch. Final existence evidence requires materially separated starts with distinct provenance, dense fixed-λ polishing of the unchanged coupled equations, every unchanged residual gate, strict positive flow, and bounded scaled-state agreement. The optimizer's internal status is diagnostic, not an engineering gate.

**Why:** Sparse iterative correctors and the Picard epsilon floor can stop just outside a gate or force continuation backward. Treating either outcome as nonexistence—or treating one corrected point as reproducible—overclaims the numerical evidence.

**How to apply:** Keep literal zero feeds unchanged and never add trace solvent. Distinguish the Picard numerical floor from the governing `flow > 0` domain, audit the full Jacobian at two finite-difference scales, use a beyond-boundary margin at least as wide as the terminal bracket, and downgrade to unresolved unless both independent fixed-λ confirmations agree and pass.
