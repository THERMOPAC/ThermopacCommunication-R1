---
name: ECR-2 recovery-constrained height
description: Design-height feasibility requires the aromatic product target and RRBO recovery together.
---

An ECR-2 physical height is a feasible preliminary design point only when the full counter-current BVP converges, global mass balance passes, hydrocarbon-only raffinate aromatics meet their target, and NMP-free RRBO mass recovery is at least 0.95. NMP is excluded from both RRBO-recovery numerator and denominator.

**Why:** A taller column can improve aromatic removal by transferring enough RRBO to the extract that the product target alone is misleading. A real persisted ECR-2 case has no feasible evaluated diameter/height point under this joint requirement.

**How to apply:** Keep recovery-limited outcomes fail-closed: persist all evaluated trial diagnostics and the best recovery-qualified aromatic result, but never retain a selected BVP, active height, or feasible D/H point. Do not change the physical equations to recover feasibility.
