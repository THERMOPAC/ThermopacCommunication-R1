---
name: Wet seven-component qualification roadmap
description: Governing sequence and admission boundaries for replacing the unqualified NMP/H2O thermodynamic model.
---

Production admission follows this fixed sequence: acquire wet LLE evidence; freeze calibration, held-out, and blind partitions; fit a stability-constrained explicit seven-component model; qualify one wet equilibrium contact; pass held-out and blind wet validation; freeze a new immutable engine; qualify N_T = 1–10; qualify sulfur independently; then admit production use.

**Why:** Model topology, thermodynamic stability, and predictive validation are separate gates. A plausible split can be unstable, a stable model can fit poorly, and a fitted model can fail blind wet prediction. Cascade tuning cannot repair a Gibbs-energy surface that does not provide the required physical LLE.

**How to apply:** Distinguish overall feed from equilibrium phase compositions and declare every phase-amount basis. Require nontrivial splitting only for records experimentally inside the two-phase region. Give phase-fraction error its own governed metric and tolerance. Reject unexplained oscillation, discontinuity, or branch switching rather than requiring every component profile to be monotonic. Keep sulfur NOT_CALCULABLE until sulfur-family representation and evidence exist.
