name: Kühni paper audit boundary
description: Missing equation extraction must not be mistaken for absence of a literature mixing correlation.
---

Audit original equations before claiming the stored papers contain no predictive axial-mixing route. Asadollahzadeh (2017), model section Eq. 9, explicitly invokes a continuous-phase axial-mixing correlation; its algebra is blank in the stored text extraction. Its reference is Kumar–Hartland (1992), for rotating-disc and asymmetric rotating-disc columns, so transfer to Kühni must be assessed rather than silently accepted.

**Why:** Earlier advice conflated an unimplemented or unreadable correlation with nonexistent evidence, and incorrectly suggested pilot measurements were a prerequisite for all pre-pilot calculations.

**How to apply:** Recover and visually verify the source equation and definitions, distinguish correlation extrapolation from a universal constant, and label uncertainty. Laitinen (2019) Table 3 provides continuous-phase dispersion values for its own experiments; the reported 20–30% backflow is a specific CFD observation, not a transferable default. Inspect primary typography: its Eq. 10 prints a suspicious resistance denominator, and Table 2 interfacial-tension units conflict with the measurement text. Do not silently transcribe either.
