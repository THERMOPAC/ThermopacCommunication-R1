description: Thermodynamic extract/raffinate identity is distinct from hydraulic continuous/dispersed assignment.

Stage-2 equilibrium admission must support both canonical continuous/dispersed orientations while preserving the selected orientation in immutable upstream authority.

**Why:** The ideal equilibrium cascade calculates extract and raffinate thermodynamic phases, not hydraulic phase continuity. An orientation-only change does not change its thermodynamic feed, solvent, or target inputs. Rejecting that change conflates hydrodynamic applicability with equilibrium.

**How to apply:** Keep extract/raffinate labels fixed and map them to continuous/dispersed only where required downstream. Do not infer hydraulic or transfer qualification for the reversed orientation from Stage-2 admission. Preserve separate snapshot identity even when thermodynamic numerical inputs are identical.
