---
name: ECR R1 construction evidence boundary
description: Primary construction evidence, free-area ambiguity and terminal-count limits for automatic Kühni geometry.
---

The active pre-pilot hydraulic basis is a composite correlation chain, not a single complete construction specification. Garthe's dissertation explicitly distinguishes ring stators from Fang's perforated stators and warns that stator-shape and compartment-height effects are confounded (printed pp.86–87). Equal free-area fractions do not establish hydraulic equivalence.

The geometry envelopes hc/D=0.20–0.30, Dr/D=0.33–0.50 and stator free area=0.20–0.40 have a verified handbook basis: Perry 8th edition, printed p.15-83, explicitly attributes these recommended Kühni scale-up factors to Pratt and Stevens, Science and Practice of Liquid-Liquid Extraction, vol.1, Chapter 8, p.541. The actual Perry page was inspected; the original Pratt/Stevens page was not independently accessed.

**Why:** Those scale-up recommendations differ from K&H1995's pooled experimental ratios. Finding the optimizer outside K&H's geometry envelope does not establish that its bounds were invented or that the handbook recommendations are invalid.

**How to apply:** Separate recommended design/scale-up envelopes from parameter-wise experimental coverage and joint correlation validity. K&H marginal bounds are not a proven rectangular set of jointly tested combinations. Sulzer's public descriptions support adjustable geometry but do not supply these numerical ratio bounds.

**Why:** R1 must define a traceable construction rather than treating optimizer ratios or pooled apparatus dimensions as approved fabrication proportions.

**How to apply:** Garthe (TUM record 601973; repository year 2006), Figs.4.9/5.17 and Table A.1, documents a six-blade ring-stator apparatus. Weber & Jupke (2020), DOI 10.1002/aic.16286, §2.1 and Figs.1–2, provides additional dimensioned geometry attributed to Garthe, including a stepped blade/web rather than an ordinary rectangular blade. Use this as apparatus-specific evidence, not a universal scale-up rule. Root/attachment dimensions require careful figure interpretation.

Do not declare the Kühni shaft-blockage convention proven by either the K&H rotating-disc parenthetical or rounding of Garthe's nominal free area.

**Why:** K&H1995 p.3937 explicitly attaches (Ds/Dc)^2 to rotating-disc columns. Garthe calls phi_s relative free stator area without an explicit shaft formula. Weber/Jupke's nominal 80-mm column, 50-mm opening and 10-mm shaft imply gross/gross 0.390625 versus shaft-subtracted/gross 0.375; closeness to nominal 0.40 favours gross/gross but rounding alone cannot uniquely establish the convention.

**How to apply:** Report gross geometric opening, shaft-blocked physical opening and empirical correlation input separately until the exact mapping is approved. Record remaining ambiguity rather than silently subtracting shaft area.

Terminal plate counts must distinguish source statements from topology deductions.

**Why:** Weber/Jupke explicitly states 53 stirred sections; its drawings show shared ring boundaries and end zones, but do not explicitly state a total stator count or a universal N+1 rule.

**How to apply:** An N+1 reconstruction requires ring stators at both active-stack ends and shared internal separators. Label that apparatus-specific geometric inference, not a quoted plate count or a general Kühni requirement.
