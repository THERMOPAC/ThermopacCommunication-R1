Drag Relationships.
Relationships developed on
the basis of relative-motion correlations for multiparticle
systems and friction in a packed bed fall in this category.
Multiparticle Systems. Using extended definitions
of the drag coefficient and Reynolds numbers for mul-
tiparticle systems, the hold-up data for spray columns
have successfully been correlated (Bamea and Mizrahi,
1975; Ishii and Zuber, 1979; Kumar and Hartland,
1985). Furthermore, it has been possible to extend this
approach to other types of columns (Kumar and Hart-
