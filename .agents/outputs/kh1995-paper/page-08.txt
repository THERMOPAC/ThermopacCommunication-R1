Comparison with Published Correlations
The forms of published equations dictate various
restrictions to be imposed on their application, although
such limitations have not always explicitly been men-
tioned in the original references. Wherever possible,
data selection criteria for literature equations have been
formulated in the present work.
These are
given in
Tables 5—11, which compare the values of and Vsiip
predicted by various correlations with those predicted
by eq 15 in terms of the average absolute value of the
relative error
for different types of columns.

always been recommended or examined for the predic-
tion of hold-up in various types of columns. A selected
number of them, however,
were
tested to ascertain
whether these simple approaches could be extended to
columns other than that for which they were
derived.
