Packed Columns.
The data set considered in this
work is the same
as that used by Kumar and Hartland
(1994a). Important details are listed below.
(1) The data for the conditions when the continuous
phase wets the packing only are considered.
(2) Only those random-packing data for which the
packing size, dp, is greater than the critical packing size,
dp,cr, are selected. The critical packing size is calculated
by using the equation suggested by Gayler et al. (1953).
(3) The data corresponding to the regions of linearly
increasing and rapidly increasing hold-up only
are
considered, and those corresponding to constant hold-
up region are
omitted.
Further information on this
selection of data is given in Kumar and Hartland
(1994a).
The total number of data points is 2023, including 672
data with mass transfer. Unfortunately, data from only
56 experiments corresponding to ordered packing are
available. The random packings used in the available
experimental results comprised Raschig rings, Pall
Rings, Bialecki rings, Berl saddles, Intalox saddles, and
spheres. The ordered packings studied are Sulzer BX,
Sulzer SMV, and Montz pack.
