The single correlation is developed to apply in all 
the three operating regimes discussed above. The 
equation is based on 16 different liquid-liquid 
systems and 326 published drop size measurements 
(Kumar and Hartland, 1986). The effect of system 
geometry, 
pulsation 
intensity, 
and 
physical 
properties of phases is included. The effect of phase 
superficial velocities is not incorporated.
