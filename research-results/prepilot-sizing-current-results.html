Physical compartments and efficiency

The recorded hardware relation is compartment pitch = 0.5 × diameter. The seven finite-volume cells and ten configured equilibrium stages are not physical compartments. The current theoretical-stage authority is the explicitly labelled pre-pilot default NT = 7, not a calculated NT.

A physical integer count must satisfy the full duty at its exact hardware height. Efficiency is then calculated as NT/physical count, retaining that theoretical-stage provenance—not assumed to obtain a height.

Numerical closeout

The completed 33-node local trial converged, with full reduced rank 391/391 and nonnegative Bernstein film profiles. Its independent constitutive defects were 1.648e-8 (continuous) and 1.834e-7 (dispersed), above the unchanged 1e-8 limit. A later driver result-processing NameError stopped the attempt after 338.12 seconds; this was not budget exhaustion or a physical-instability finding. That processing error was fixed and three native-free regression tests passed, but no further physical run was performed. Two qualifying successive refinements, independent final-root reproduction, exact-candidate stability and whole-column sizing remain uncompleted.

Sulfur remains a separate qualification
