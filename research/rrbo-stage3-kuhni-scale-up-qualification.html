RRBO/NMP Stage-3 Kühni hydraulic scale-up qualification

Engineering qualification report · diagnostic and pre-pilot basis · 2026-09-15

Final decision

Case B industrial scale-up status, 0.693099697 m: B — major scale-up extrapolation, but not an invalid design. This is a pre-pilot estimate: the current 70%-loading root of the illustrative reverse-orientation hydraulic model. It is not a proof of physical feasibility or a governed commercial diameter. Perry's explicit recommendation maps a 60 mm pilot to columns up to 1 m, so the target is not prohibited; the model remains a major extrapolation because its compartment ratio is outside the published recommendation, its reverse orientation is unresolved, and its viscosity ratio is below the reviewed Myint data gate.

Case A, 0.974212919 m, has the same category B. It is also a pre-pilot estimate below Perry's 1 m boundary for the 60 mm pilot mapping, but is beyond individual apparatus evidence and is not a proof of physical feasibility; it uses the same out-of-range hc/D=0.50.

A pilot is not required to produce the present pre-pilot prediction and no fixed pilot flow is assumed in the pilot invariant rows. Pilot/vendor evidence is required to upgrade the category to a defensible commercial design status; a pilot is not a prerequisite merely to produce this prediction.

Required confirmation before commercial design

  1. Vendor drawing: ID, active height, compartment height, rotor/blade/shroud, shaft, stator type/free area, clearances, ports, seals, torque, power, rpm, and tip-speed limits.
  2. Phase orientation and interface control, start-up/shutdown, phase inversion, entrainment, and settler/outlet capacity.
  3. Full drop-size distribution, d32, breakup/coalescence, daughter populations, and dependence on speed, flow ratio, temperature, σ, and composition.
  4. Terminal and compartment/swarm slip in the actual phase pair, including circulation, stator/wall interaction, and holdup.
  5. Throughput/speed map for holdup, flooding onset, entrainment, pressure/interface behavior, and hysteresis.
  6. A scale-up record stating the selected geometry and the reason for any chosen invariant. No fixed pilot flow is needed for the pre-pilot invariant calculations, but capacity validation requires actual operating data.

Final disposition

Case B industrial scale-up status is B — major scale-up extrapolation, but not an invalid design. The current 0.693099697 m value remains an illustrative pre-pilot hydraulic prediction, not proof of physical feasibility. Perry's 60 mm-to-1 m recommendation and Sulzer's 30 mm–3.5 m ECR product evidence remove any claim that the diameter is prohibited, while the published hc/D=0.20–0.30 recommendation and unresolved reverse RRBO/NMP hydraulics prevent commercial qualification. The independent script and numerical JSON verify the frozen/as-coded baseline and sensitivity; the pilot invariant rows require no fixed pilot flow assumption. Rendered Garthe Eq. 8.3 places the holdup exponent inside the radical, matching the frozen resolver.
