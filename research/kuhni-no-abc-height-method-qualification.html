Efficiency and backmixing rule

An overall coefficient (Koc, Koca, or Shoc) is a rate/resistance quantity, not a Murphree stage efficiency. HETS is active height per theoretical equilibrium stage in a defined model/design basis. Neither may be converted using an arbitrary efficiency factor.

Weber et al. is cited only as an excluded complex-model comparison: it explicitly includes continuous-phase backmixing via Breysse–Bühlmann. Therefore, adding a second backmixing/Murphree penalty to an HETS from that configured model would double count. It is not recommended as an alternative solver.
