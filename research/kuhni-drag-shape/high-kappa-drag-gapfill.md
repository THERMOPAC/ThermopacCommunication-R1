**Explicit verdict: NO-GO for Garthe Eq. 8.3.** I found no primary relation that simultaneously provides liquid-in-liquid drag, arbitrary supplied characteristic/swarm-slip Re, deformation dependence, no system fitting, and defensible coverage at κ=33.6 and Mo=2.86e-9.

Key evidence:
- **Barry & Parlange (2018):** closest unfitted primary arbitrary-Re relation; supports all viscosity ratios (thus κ=33.6) to Re of a few hundred, but explicitly assumes a spherical, non-wobbling particle. Mo is not an input.
- **Saboni–Alexandrova:** direct Cd(Re,κ), 0.01≤Re<400; κ=33.6 is evaluable, but the relation is spherical and deformation-blind.
- **Hamielec/Rivkind–Ryskin/Brauer:** arbitrary-Re fluid-sphere laws only; no Eo, We, Mo, or aspect ratio.
- **Wellek:** shape only, E=b/a=[1+0.163Eo^0.757]^-1; no Cd or Re dependence. Pairing it with solid-oblate drag is a new unsupported composite.
- **Thorsen and Grace:** deformation/oscillation-aware terminal-velocity correlations only. Grace’s stated Mo<10^-3 includes the target Mo, but it cannot evaluate Cd at independently supplied swarm-slip Re.
- **Henschke/ReDrop:** deformation-aware terminal-velocity crossover, but requires fitted transition/oscillation parameters; not an arbitrary-slip Cd closure.
- **Taylor–Acrivos / Dandy–Leal / oblate computations:** asymptotic or coupled solution families, not a source-native closed arbitrary-Re constitutive law.
