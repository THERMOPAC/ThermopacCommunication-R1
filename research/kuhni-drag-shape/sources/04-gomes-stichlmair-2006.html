The possibility of testing very complex theoretical models of such behavior is increasingly attractive to the researcher, as a result of both the power and the availability of personal computing resources. Experimental data obtained in a pilot-scale Kühni liquid−liquid extraction column and simulated data generated by means of a drop population-balance model and algorithm were used to describe and compute the local drop-size distributions and dispersed-phase holdup profiles. In this work, the applicability of this algorithm to describe the steady-state behavior of a Kühni liquid−liquid extraction column is illustrated.
