🛠️ Minor Logical/Structural Adjustments Suggested
1. Advance Payment Allocation Timing
"System checks for unallocated advance payments from same customer. If advance payments exist, they can be automatically allocated to the new invoice."

✅ Suggest being more explicit:

"System checks for unallocated advance payments from the same customer at the time of invoice creation. If found, they are either auto-applied (based on rules) or left for manual allocation."

Why? It’s important to clarify whether this is auto or user-controlled to avoid confusion.

2. Outstanding Calculation Formula
"Invoice outstanding = original amount - sum of all allocations - write-offs"

✅ Correct logic, but might benefit from emphasizing the update direction.

Better phrased as:

"Each time a payment is applied or a write-off is recorded, the system recalculates invoice and payment balances:"

invoice.outstanding = invoice.amount - allocations - write-offs

payment.unallocated = payment.amount - allocations - write-offs

3. Write-Off Logic Slightly Blended
You wrote:

"Write-offs can go through approval workflow or be auto-approved below threshold"
"System maintains complete audit trail of all write-offs"

✅ Suggest grouping it more clearly:

Invoice Write-Off

Payment Write-Off

This makes the write-off process clearer and separates scenarios (e.g., excess invoice or excess payment) rather than treating all residuals uniformly.

4. Payment Receipt → Allocation
"Payment is recorded with full unallocated amount. If marked as advance... Otherwise, payment can be immediately allocated..."

✅ This is good, but consider clarifying system support for:

Allocating at the time of payment creation vs

Allocating later via a separate allocation screen

That helps developers/designers know if both are supported in UI/backend.

5. Multi-currency Notes
Mention is good, but it would be even better to include:

"Exchange rate at the time of allocation is recorded and used for accurate outstanding reconciliation."

If this isn’t implemented yet, this might be a roadmap point.
