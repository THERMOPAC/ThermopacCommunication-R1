### Task Title: Prasad - Enhance Meeting Engagement and Commitment Follow-through

**Task Description:**

- **Personal Issue:** Low engagement and commitment follow-through in meetings you organize.
  
- **Impact on You:** This affects your productivity and reputation by potentially leading to missed opportunities for collaboration and project advancement. It may also impact your perceived reliability and leadership effectiveness.

- **Your Pattern:** You have organized 29 meetings but have not attended any as a participant in the last 30 days. Additionally, your commitment creation and completion rates are at 0%, indicating a lack of follow-through on meeting outcomes.

- **Personal Action:**
  1. **Increase Engagement:** Start attending at least one meeting as a participant per week to gain diverse perspectives and improve collaboration skills.
  2. **Set Clear Objectives:** For each meeting you organize, establish clear objectives and expected outcomes. Communicate these to participants before the meeting.
  3. **Track Commitments:** Begin documenting commitments made during meetings. Use a task management tool to track and remind yourself of these commitments.
  4. **Follow-up Strategy:** Implement a follow-up strategy post-meeting to ensure commitments are being addressed. This could include setting reminders or scheduling brief check-ins with team members.
  5. **Feedback Loop:** Seek feedback from participants on meeting effectiveness and your leadership style. Use this feedback to make iterative improvements.

- **Your Success Metric:** 
  - Achieve a 50% commitment creation rate in the next month.
  - Improve your commitment completion rate to at least 25% within the next two months.
  - Receive positive feedback from at least 70% of meeting participants regarding meeting effectiveness and your leadership style.

By focusing on these areas, you can enhance your meeting effectiveness, improve team productivity, and strengthen your leadership reputation.
