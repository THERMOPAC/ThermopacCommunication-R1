RRBO-continuous / wet-NMP-dispersed reverse-hydraulic scientific audit

Audit mode: read-only, before recalculation. No hydraulic solve or optimizer replay; no database query; no application, database, drawing, memory, or scientific-input change. Evidence is source code, tests, prior audits, and visually inspected retained primary-source pages.

1. Executive disposition

Scientific status: mathematically calculable, empirically unqualified. Signed buoyancy and countercurrent kinematics are defensible necessary physics. Preliminary d32, Myint drag/shape, Garthe characteristic velocity, swarm/holdup, turning-point capacity, diameter, and RPM remain a linked extrapolated chain. Reversal is not automatically invalid.

Using |Δρ| in buoyancy, Eötvös, Morton, Archimedes, or drag-speed magnitude equations can be valid once direction is retained independently. The current reverse kernel restores the negative sign to relative velocity and reports downward dispersed motion. Magnitudes do not, however, validate drift routing, interface behavior, or an empirical model's domain.

Two-level admission finding: V1.4/V1.5 exclude reverse roots from the governed hydraulic envelope, while the presentation layer intentionally permits an independently rechecked root for non-governed HETS-only screening. The current optimizer likewise treats correlation evidence as metadata, marks numerically acceptable extrapolations FEASIBLE for preliminary screening, and supplies Stage-4 screening geometry. Project policy expressly permits this behavior and applies assumption-based Stage 4 to both orientations. It is not established as a code bug; it is also not scientific qualification.

The reviewed contracts distinguish operational screening acceptance from empirical qualification. Phase-1 policy states evidence status is metadata rather than an execution gate; the hydraulic-chain policy permits a complete chain to remain CALCULATED_EXTRAPOLATED; Stage 4 is labeled PRE-PILOT PREDICTIVE / SCREENING DESIGN, uses an adopted non-performance efficiency, and denies outlet/final-design claims. Tests intentionally allow either a selected reverse screening result or no bounded result, but prohibit silent orientation substitution.

Gap relative to this audit's stronger objective: there is no positive field establishing that the complete reverse chain is scientifically qualified or release eligible. Generic FEASIBLE, SELECTED, hydraulicPass, and empty numerical blockers must be interpreted only at the approved screening level.

The existing 1.3 m candidate is comparison-only. It must not be described as a qualified selection or operating guarantee.

3.5 Swarm/hindrance

vs/vchar=sqrt[(Cd(Rechar)/Cd(Res))(1-h)^4.65]

Visual inspection of Garthe printed p.126 Eq. 8.3 confirms the radical covers the entire product. The fixed 4.65 is a drag/swarm hindrance relation connected to Richardson–Zaki/rigid-sphere behavior; because it is under the radical, the constant-drag velocity exponent is 2.325. It is not a universal liquid-drop exponent. Reusing one Myint drag function at terminal, characteristic, and swarm states is an unvalidated composite assumption.

3.6 Turning-point “flooding” and loading

h_flood=arg max jT(h); Uflood=max jT(h)
actualLoading=(Qc+Qd)/(A Uflood)

This is a reproducible maximum of the assumed slip closure, not independently observed flooding. It does not establish the lower stable operating branch, entrainment, inversion, hysteresis, or disengagement limits.

Semantic defect: the reverse optimizer maps record.floodHoldup into candidate holdup and computes 6h/d32 from it (stage3-stage4-optimizer.ts:643-645). That is flood-point holdup, not operating φ. Ordinary K&H operating holdup and reverse flood holdup must not be conflated.

3.7 Search feasibility versus qualification

Reverse trials become FEASIBLE from finite state, force closure, tip speed, and loading ≤0.7 (stage3-stage4-optimizer.ts:606-657). Contiguous windows and the second-smallest adequate-diameter ranking then operate on those flags. These are search-feasible flags only. A 20 rpm window is a ranking preference, not a hydraulic limit or validation.

6. Exact reason for “unqualified”

  • Missing evidence: reverse drop population; breakup/coalescence; falling low-κ interface/shape; forced-slip drag; reverse holdup branch; flooding/entrainment/inversion; ports/control; vendor mechanical and scale-up basis.
  • Two-level policy: known limitations are intentionally nonblocking for approved preliminary screening. That does not provide a positive scientific-qualification or release verdict.
  • Model applicability: terminal-drop, characteristic-slip, swarm, and flooding closures are distinct gates. Passing scalar ranges or numerical closure does not qualify their composition.

8. Defensible present use

The model may report signed direction, bulk-property mapping, assumed power and d32 sensitivity, extrapolated terminal/shape/characteristic/swarm diagnostics, the assumed capacity maximum, loading, bounded numerical failures, comparison candidates, and—under approved policy—a selected preliminary screening diameter/RPM window and assumption-based Stage-4 screening geometry. At the scientific-qualification or release level it may not authorize operating holdup/interfacial area, qualified flooding, pilot-validated diameter/RPM, scientifically qualified/final Stage-4 geometry, vendor feasibility, or separation performance.

9. Controls before claiming scientific qualification

  1. Persist separate numericallyCalculable, screeningFeasible, scientificallyQualified, and releaseEligible flags.
  2. Preserve approved preliminary screening, but require a positive versioned qualification object before any scientifically-qualified, pilot-validated, or release claim.
  3. Retain comparison roots under explicitly non-governing labels.
  4. Stop substituting floodHoldup for operating φ.
  5. Require a positive, versioned scientific-admission object in any qualified/release consumer; do not retroactively apply it to the explicitly approved screening consumer.
  6. Keep alternate orientation comparison-only; never silently switch orientation.
  7. Preserve warnings through persistence, API compaction, and display.

10. Exact promotion requirements

  1. Qualified viscous reverse d32/distribution, breakup/coalescence, geometry/power scaling, and uncertainty.
  2. Low-κ falling-drop interface, contamination, deformation, and drag/shape evidence.
  3. Applicable terminal and forced characteristic/swarm slip closure, including wall/rotor/stator effects.
  4. Operating lower holdup branch, multiplicity/hysteresis, observed or validated flooding, entrainment, inversion, and approved margin.
  5. Verified distributors, collection, interface control, ports, start-up/shutdown, vendor geometry, torque, RPM, tip speed, and scale-up rule.
  6. Versioned governance approval and regression tests preventing preliminary reverse results from being presented as scientifically qualified or release-eligible, while retaining expressly approved Stage-4 preliminary screening.

11. Final conclusion

Reverse operation is not invalid merely because wet NMP is denser. The signed force and proposed countercurrent mapping are coherent. The full empirical chain remains unqualified. Current optimizer and Stage-4 behavior is intentional preliminary-screening admission under the reviewed two-level policy, not a demonstrated code bug. No positive scientific-release gate exists, so its generic status words must remain scoped to screening. Independently, mapping floodHoldup into candidate operating holdup and interfacial area is a concrete semantic/model mismatch that should be corrected or relabeled. The 1.3 m comparison and reverse RPM windows are not scientifically qualified selections.
