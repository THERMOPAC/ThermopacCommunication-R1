Implemented chain

  1. Consume the saved 40 °C properties unchanged. RRBO continuous/upward, wet NMP dispersed/downward.
  2. Power = 1.2 ρc N³ Dr⁵; ε = Power/(A hc ρc). Conditional d32 = C(σ/ρc)0.6 ε−0.4, with C=.36, .42 and .43. This is a Sauter-mean proxy, not a maximum stable diameter.
  3. Barry–Parlange Eq. 10 mobile and Schiller–Naumann immobile scenarios both solve Cd Re² = 4Ar/3. Barry–Parlange uses its positive τ root and primary-source coefficients. No interface is assumed to be actual.
  4. Garthe Eqs. 5.6–5.7 supply characteristic velocity; the source power number is distinct from fixed engineering Np=1.2.
  5. Garthe Eq. 8.3: vs/v0 = √[(Cd0/Cds)(1−φ)4.65]. Slip = vs/(1−φ) before capacity or operating balances. Low-Re superficial exponent is 4.65; slip exponent is 3.65.
  6. Actual operating balance: jd/φ + jc/(1−φ) = vslip. Retain the lower dilute-connected quasi-steady branch, not a claim of dynamical stability.
  7. Modeled capacity maximizes (1+r)vslip/[r/φ+1/(1−φ)], with separate flood holdup. Interfacial area is 6φop/d32, never calculated from flood holdup. No root means null operating holdup and area.
  8. Take the lower capacity over all six interface/coefficient scenarios; all must have roots and loading≤.70 to pass screening, alongside the existing tip-speed ceiling.

Numerics and scientific limits

Bounded 70-iteration bisection, log-velocity swarm brackets, 512 holdup intervals, 60 capacity-refinement iterations and 16 proportional flow-continuation steps are explicit implementation assumptions. All sign-changing mesh brackets are refined. A finite mesh is not proof that arbitrarily narrow additional branches cannot exist. Missing or non-monotone lower roots and turning-point contact do not become operating states.

Inversion, entrainment, disengagement, turbulence, sphericity, SN drag-range qualification and actual interface mobility remain independently UNKNOWN. Passing loading does not qualify them. No authoritative numerical regime threshold has been invented.

Output includes rotor, terminal, characteristic and swarm Reynolds numbers; Eo; terminal We; both Oh numbers; power and ε; signed phase velocities; force/operating residuals; roots and continuation; source-range ratios and all scenarios.
