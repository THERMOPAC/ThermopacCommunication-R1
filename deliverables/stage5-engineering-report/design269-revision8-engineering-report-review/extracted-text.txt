Residence calculation and closure holds
Ten-minute hold-up: V10 = Qnormal / 6, with Qnormal in m³/h. Usable straight volume = 0.90 × 
(πD²/4) × H10. Therefore H10 = Qnormal / [6 × 0.90 × (πD²/4)].
This is one relationship between diameter and height. It does not uniquely choose both, even when 
normal flow and product density are qualified. No economic optimum, aspect ratio, maximum height 
or arbitrary candidate diameter is introduced.
