## Abstract

Experimental data obtained in a pilot-scale Kühni liquid−liquid extraction column were used to obtain local drop size distributions, dispersed-phase hold-up profiles, and solute concentrations. To obtain information about the dynamic behavior of the column, several experiments were planned and performed, under both steady and controlled transient conditions, with and without mass transfer. The analysis of the effects of the presence of a transferring solute (not of the mass transfer flux itself) on the dispersion’s steady state and dynamic behavior was the main objective. For this, we imposed forced step variations in the operating conditions − agitation intensity and continuous and dispersed-phase flowrates.

**Subjects** [Surface tension](https://pubs.acs.org/search-results?page=1&tax=1097), [Extraction](https://pubs.acs.org/search-results?page=1&tax=21), [Hydrodynamics](https://pubs.acs.org/search-results?page=1&tax=3439), [Mass transfer](https://pubs.acs.org/search-results?page=1&tax=576), [Solution chemistry](https://pubs.acs.org/search-results?page=1&tax=664)
