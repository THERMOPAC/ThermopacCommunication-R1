1. One recommended chain

Conditional recommendation: retain the direct turbulent d32 scaling as an interval-valued project screening closure; replace governing low-κ Myint drag with Barry–Parlange spherical fluid-drop drag; retain Garthe’s Kühni characteristic and Eq. 8.3 swarm relations; convert Garthe superficial swarm velocity to interphase slip; solve actual lower-branch holdup separately from the modeled turning-point capacity; keep phase inversion as an independent measured unknown.
  1. Fix the actual 40 °C phase/property snapshot and signed RRBO-up / wet-NMP-down convention.
  2. Use d32=C32(σ/ρc)^0.6ε^-0.4 only as a project-controlled Sauter-mean proxy. Do not call it a verified Hinze d32 or add an invented viscosity correction.
  3. Carry two mandatory interface scenarios through the full chain: Barry–Parlange Eq. 10 for a spherical clean/mobile drop and Schiller–Naumann for an immobile spherical interface. Until mobility is known, use the lower predicted capacity as the conservative envelope.
  4. Use Garthe Eqs. 5.6–5.7 and 8.3. Use the accepted drag law consistently at both Reynolds states.
  5. Solve jd/φ+jc/(1-φ)=vslip(φ) and select the quasi-steady admissible root continuously connected to the dilute lower branch; do not claim dynamical stability.
  6. Compute modeled turning capacity by a separate maximization. Never substitute flood holdup for operating holdup.
  7. Constrain later operation by independently measured inversion, entrainment, disengagement, interface-control and hardware limits.

Not implementation-ready: numeric turbulence, spherical/non-wobbling and Schiller–Naumann range gates, plus root mesh/tolerances, remain ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED. “Dependable” means auditable enough to plan a pre-pilot campaign; it does not mean validated performance, dynamical stability, guaranteed capacity, or authorization to optimize diameter/RPM.

3. Drop-size method: retain without invented correction

Dr=(Dr/D)D; hc=(hc/D)D; N=RPM/60; A=πD²/4 P=Np ρc N³ Dr⁵ ε=P/(A hc ρc) d32=C32(σ/ρc)^0.6 ε^-0.4

Units are W, W kg-1 and m. Use the current controlled C32 interval [0.36, 0.43], with 0.42 nominal only after acceptance. The interval is not demonstrated to exhaust model-form or scale-up uncertainty. Keep Np=1.2 as a separate engineering assumption. The saved/known σ=.011 N m-1 basis is not established here as a primary measurement; provenance and uncertainty remain required.

Decision

  • Retain conditionally: it is dimensionally coherent and already controlled, making it the least-inventive executable pre-pilot placeholder.
  • Do not modify: no primary source licenses a continuous-RRBO viscosity multiplier for this exact Kühni geometry and phase orientation.
  • Do not replace with a guessed K&H equation: Kumar–Hartland (1996) is bibliographically verified and covers Kühni among eight column families, but the exact primary equation/table remained unavailable.

Hinze (1955) supplies turbulent-breakup/maximum-stable-scale provenance, not this project coefficient or a universal Kühni Sauter mean. Pacek et al. (1998) show that d32 depends on the full distribution; Sathyagal et al. (1996) found continued breakup that challenges a simplistic maximum-stable-diameter interpretation. Consequently dmax and d32 must not be conflated.

Execution must fail its method-domain gate unless an accepted rotor/compartment or local-dissipation criterion demonstrates a turbulent breakup regime appropriate to the inertial scaling. This report does not invent that threshold.

4. Two mandatory isolated-drop scenarios at κ=0.02368

Barry & Parlange (2018), pp. 3–7, define Re=ρc|u|d/μc, X=μd/μc, and P=ρd/ρc. Their exact low-Re Hadamard–Rybczynski limit, Eq. 3 on printed p. 4, is:

Cd = (16/Re) (1+1.5X)/(1+X)

Their general Eq. 10 on printed p. 6 is:

Cd = [48/(Re(1+X))](1+1.5X) × [Re^0.5 + AZ + τ exp(−λRe^0.5)] / [Re^0.5/(1+X) + 3AZ + 3τ exp(−ωRe^0.5)] A=[2√2/(5√π)](6√3+5√2−14)=1.10535 Z=1+[(α−2)(XP)^0.5+(β−1)XP][1+(XP)^0.5]^-2 α=2.5891; β=0.9879 τ(τ+AZ)=(8/9)(4+3X)/(1+X); λτ=1; ωτ=1/[3(1+X)]

Use the positive root for τ. Solve the signed terminal balance:

Cd(Re,X,P) Re² = (4/3)Ar Ar = ρc(ρd−ρc)g d³/μc² ut = Re μc/(ρc d), directed downward

The source applies to a steady spherical isolated fluid particle in an infinite homogeneous liquid, all viscosity/density ratios, with comparisons to Reynolds numbers up to a few hundred. Surface tension must maintain sphericity. It does not validate contamination, deformation, walls, rotor circulation or swarms. The missing numeric spherical/non-wobbling criterion makes this specification not implementation-ready.

Mandatory mobility sensitivity

Cd,SN=(24/Re)(1+0.15Re^0.687)

At Re→0 and current X, the Barry–Parlange clean coefficient is Cd Re=16.1850; an immobile sphere has Cd Re=24. The drag ratio is 1.48285, so clean Hadamard–Rybczynski speed is up to 1.48285× rigid Stokes speed for the same spherical diameter and properties. Neither state is established as actual.

Myint is retained only as a comparator. Its rising-drop experiments state 0.1<κ<100. Carry both clean and immobile scenarios through terminal, swarm, roots and capacity; report min(jf,model,clean, jf,model,immobile) before mobility qualification.

5. Characteristic and swarm velocity

Garthe characteristic velocity

NP,G=1.08+10.94/ReR^0.5+257.37/ReR^1.5 vchar=ut[1−1.669NP,G^-3.945 −2.807(d32/(D−Dr))^1.336 −1.159(hc/D)^2.049 +2.1φs^1.032]

These are Garthe printed p. 86, Eqs. 5.6–5.7. NP,G is not the fixed engineering power number. The source reports 143 Kühni points and 10.7% relative error in a conventional-fluid pilot corpus.

Garthe Eq. 8.3

vs(φ)/v0 = { [Cd(Re0)/Cd(Res)] (1−φ)^4.65 }^0.5

Printed p. 126 confirms that the radical covers the full drag-ratio × hindrance product. Use Barry–Parlange consistently in both drag evaluations while the shape/mobility gate passes.

Definition correction verified visually: Garthe printed p. 29, Eqs. 2.37–2.38, distinguishes relative slip from superficial swarm velocity; printed p. 31 gives the laminar limit, and printed p. 126 Eq. 8.3 predicts superficial vs:

vrs = jd/φ + jc/(1−φ) vs = vrs(1−φ) therefore vslip(φ)=vs(φ)/(1−φ)

Required current-model correction: the current reverse implementation solves swarmVelocityMagnitude from Eq. 8.3 and passes it directly to capacityAtHoldup at server/ecr-pre-pilot/kuhni-geometry-resolver-v140.ts:328–343. It omits division by (1−holdup) before a relative-slip capacity expression. The proposed method must first compute vslip=vs/(1−φ). No application code is changed by this report.

At low Re, where Cd∝1/Re, Eq. 8.3 reduces to vs/v0=(1−φ)^4.65. The 2.325 exponent is only the constant-drag limit. Garthe prints both limits in Eqs. 2.42–2.43.

Garthe Figure 8.11, printed p. 139, reports 118 Kühni points, 23.5% average relative error, D=80–152 mm, agitator 45–85 mm, compartment height 5.0–7.2 cm, 28–220 min^-1, and 20–40% free area. The conventional systems do not validate RRBO/wet NMP or scale-up. The preceding validation also says drops above about 4.5 mm were not satisfactorily predicted in the cited RDC evidence.

6. Operating holdup, modeled capacity and inversion

Actual operating holdup

F(φ)=jd/φ+jc/(1−φ)−vslip(φ)=0, 0<φ<1
  1. Bracket all roots on an accepted bounded mesh.
  2. Ramp both flows proportionally from a small positive multiplier.
  3. Select φop only as the lowest-holdup root continuously connected to the dilute branch and label it quasi-steady admissible, not dynamically stable.
  4. Record every root and continuation history; treat folds/jumps as branch-loss evidence.
  5. Compute aop=6φop/d32 only from this operating root.

Separate modeled turning capacity

At fixed r=jd/jc=Qd/Qc=0.513695:

jT,allow(φ)=(1+r)vslip(φ)/[r/φ+1/(1−φ)] jf,model=max jT,allow(φ) φf,model=arg max jT,allow(φ) Lmodel=jT,actual/jf,model

φf,model is not φop. jf,model is a mathematical turning capacity, not observed flood. Evaluate both interface scenarios and use the lower modeled capacity until mobility is qualified. A fraction such as 0.70 is a separate policy margin and is not accepted here.

Separate inversion unknown

Define φinv from exact-fluid measurements. It is not φop, φf,model, automatically 0.5, or supplied by Barry–Parlange/Garthe. Until inversion, entrainment, disengagement and interface-control data exist, output only MODELED_TURNING_CAPACITY_NOT_OBSERVED_FLOOD.

7. Uncertainty and execution outputs

  1. Properties: actual-composition 40 °C density, viscosity and especially interfacial-tension uncertainty.
  2. Drop-size coefficient: evaluate C32=0.36, 0.42 and 0.43 as an epistemic interval, not a probability distribution or exhaustive uncertainty bound.
  3. Population: measure d10, d32, d43, upper tail and deformation class.
  4. Mobility: carry clean Barry–Parlange and immobile Schiller–Naumann through the full chain and conservatively envelope capacity.
  5. Extrapolation: report explicit property, geometry, speed and scale ratios; source fit error is not a current confidence interval.
  6. Branches: persist root count, continuation, turning point and distances to independently measured limits.

Historical geometry reference only: relative to Garthe maxima, prior audited D=600 mm, hc=180 mm, Dr=198 mm correspond to 600/152=3.95, 180/72=2.50, and 198/85=2.33. This quantifies source-domain distance; it is not a new solve or selected candidate.

Any later execution must emit method/acceptance IDs, property snapshot, sign convention, geometry, Np, C32, σ, numeric regime gates, d32 interval/distribution status, both interface scenarios and conservative envelope, Garthe conversion, all roots, quasi-steady φop, separate φf,model/jf,model, inversion status, and all range diagnostics. Classification remains PREPILOT_EXTRAPOLATED_METHOD.

10. Acceptance required before execution

This report does not approve the method. A versioned acceptance record must resolve coefficients, models, σ/property provenance and uncertainty, fixed Np, drop distribution, numeric turbulence/shape/range gates, two-scenario envelope, root mesh/tolerances and quasi-steady labeling, flood terminology/margin, separate inversion, and explicit scale/fluid/direction extrapolation.

Minimum pre-pilot tests at several sub-capacity points and on ramp-up and ramp-down must measure full drop-size distribution, shape, holdup, phase/slip velocities, interface behavior, entrainment, inversion onset and observed capacity. Tests must span the proposed flow-ratio and speed/power envelope.

Final disposition: RRBO_WETNMP_KUHNI_HYDRAULIC_P0 is the one recommended pre-pilot method specification, conditional on acceptance and not yet implementation-ready. It is not a Stage-3 result, final diameter/RPM selection, dynamical-stability proof, validated flood guarantee or release authorization.
